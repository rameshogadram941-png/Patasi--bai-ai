# Patasi Bai AI — Brand & Mission

Brand name (public): Patasi Bai AI
Operating entity (draft): Patasi Bai AI (Patasi Bai AI Foundation for legal/registration)

Mission

Patasi Bai AI empowers women, vulnerable communities, and displaced people across India and neighbouring countries by providing vernacular AI tools, training, and market access that generate sustainable income and build local resilience.

Vision

A world where every woman and marginalized person can use simple, affordable AI to earn, learn, and thrive — starting in India, expanding to Nepal and other high‑need regions, and partnering with humanitarian organizations to scale impact.

Founding principles
- Local-first: vernacular, voice/WhatsApp-first UI, low-bandwidth friendly.
- Dignity: respect beneficiaries’ agency; provide tools that increase income and independence.
- Evidence-driven: run pilots, measure income uplift, publish impact reports.
- Privacy & safety: minimal data collection, human-in-loop for sensitive advice.
- Sustainability: hybrid funding — grants + paid services to reinvest in programs.
