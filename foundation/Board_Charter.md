# Board Charter — Patasi Bai AI Foundation

Purpose
Provide governance, strategic oversight, fiduciary responsibility, and accountability for the Foundation’s mission.

Board composition (suggested)
- Chair (external, governance lead)
- Executive Director (day-to-day leader; can be ex-officio)
- Treasurer (finance oversight)
- 2–4 independent directors (philanthropy, tech, humanitarian, legal)

Key responsibilities
- Approve strategic plan, annual budget, and major partnerships
- Review and sign off on pilot evaluations and impact reports
- Ensure compliance, safeguarding, and data protection

Meetings & cadence
- Quarterly board meetings (minimum)
- Annual General Meeting

Conflict of interest
- Directors must declare conflicts and recuse themselves.

-- End charter --
