Objective:
Provide safe, low‑bandwidth livelihood tools for women and displaced people in conflict or displacement contexts (Nepal, border regions) and create immediate earning opportunities via micro‑tasks and digital freelancing.

- WhatsApp/IVR first onboarding in Nepali and Hindi
- Livelihood matching: produce-based crafts, small services, digital micro‑tasks

Success metrics (3 months):
- 300 beneficiaries onboarded
- 200 active income‑generating tasks completed
- Average income target: NPR/INR equivalent ₹1,500 per user/month

Budget (indicative): INR 10,00,000 (or equivalent donor grant)

Safeguards & ethics:
- No collection of sensitive identity data without consent
- Coordinate with Red Cross / local authorities for safety
- Referral pathways for protection, medical and psychosocial help
