Pilot Onboarding Checklist — Patasi Bai AI Foundation

1) Partnership kickoff
- Confirm partner contact and sign MoU template
- Agree on pilot dates, venue, and local coordinator

2) Beneficiary selection
- Select 100–200 beneficiaries (priority: women sellers / SHG members)
- Capture minimal baseline data: name, phone, primary product/service, monthly income
- Obtain written/verbal consent (use provided consent form)

3) Logistics
- Arrange two half-day training sessions (venue, projector / phone charging)
- Ensure 1–2 local trainers available
- Provide small stipend / airtime or SIM support as agreed

4) Tech setup
- Ensure coordinator has a smartphone and WhatsApp access
- Provide trainer with admin access to the onboarding dashboard (provided in repo)

5) Monitoring & evaluation
- Baseline survey (use M&E_baseline_template.csv)
- Weekly progress check-ins (photo evidence of listings, sample transactions)
- Endline survey at 12 weeks

6) Safeguarding & referrals
- Use referral pathways for protection, health, or psychosocial needs
- Avoid collecting sensitive identity info; anonymize where possible

7) Reporting
- Monthly status report to Foundation
- Final impact report with topline KPIs and lessons learned
