Trainer Script — Two Half-Day Sessions (outline)

Session 1 (Half-day) — Onboarding & Listing Creation
- Welcome & introductions (15 min)
- Explain program and consent (15 min)
- WhatsApp basics & safety (20 min)
- Demo: take photo -> generate listing (title, description, price) using Patasi AI (20 min)
- Hands-on: participants create 1–2 listings with trainer support (60 min)
- Create simple social caption and share to WhatsApp groups (30 min)
- Close & homework: take 3 product photos and prepare for session 2 (10 min)

Session 2 (Half-day) — Payments, Invoicing & Selling
- Recap and show best examples from homework (20 min)
- Demo: generate WhatsApp invoice + Razorpay UPI link and send to buyer (20 min)
- Roleplay: buyer-seller conversation and closing sale (40 min)
- Using captions and creatives for social selling (30 min)
- Troubleshooting common issues & next steps (20 min)
- Collect feedback and schedule follow-ups (10 min)
