Title: Scaling Vernacular AI Tools for Women & Displaced Communities — Pilot (India & Nepal)

Ask: USD 75,000 (approx INR 60 Lakh)

Purpose:
Fund a 6‑month pilot across 3 regions (1 urban women seller cluster, 1 KVK/agri cluster, 1 refugee/NGO cluster in Nepal) to deliver vernacular AI tools (WhatsApp/IVR), trainings, and market linkages that increase income for 1,000 beneficiaries.

Expected outcomes:
- 1,000 beneficiaries onboarded and trained
- Average income uplift of ₹1,500–2,500 per beneficiary/month
- Evidence package including baseline/endline surveys, case studies, and dashboard

Budget summary:
- Tech & platform: $20,000
- Field operations & training: $30,000
- Monitoring & evaluation: $10,000
- Admin & contingency: $15,000

Partners: Patasi Bai AI Foundation (implementer), local NGOs/SHG partners, KVKs (agri)
