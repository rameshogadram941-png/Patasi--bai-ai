# Pitch Deck (10 Slides) — Patasi Bai AI

Slide 1 — Title
Patasi Bai AI — Vernacular AI for Women & Vulnerable Communities

Slide 2 — Problem
Millions of women and displaced people lack low-cost tools to sell, learn, and access markets. Language, literacy and connectivity barriers prevent adoption.

Slide 3 — Solution
Patasi Bai AI: WhatsApp/IVR-first vernacular AI tools that generate listings, invoices, lesson plans and market links — low friction and mobile-first.

Slide 4 — Impact Model
Kits: Chanakya (business), Tenali (marketing), Abdul Kalam (education), Birbal (dispute), Vivekananda (empowerment). Pilot → scale via NGOs, KVKs, CSR, UNICEF & Red Cross.

Slide 5 — Traction (Pilot Plan)
3 pilots: Women sellers, Farmer women, Refugee livelihoods (Nepal). Target 1,000 beneficiaries in 6 months.

Slide 6 — Technology
Hybrid inference: local models (Ollama) + cloud LLMs; RAG for factual checks; multilingual stack (Hindi, Nepali, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali).

Slide 7 — Partnerships
Local NGOs/SHGs, KVKs, Razorpay/Stripe for payments, Meta WhatsApp Cloud, UNICEF/Red Cross for humanitarian scale.

Slide 8 — Budget & Ask
Seed ask: INR 60 Lakh (USD 75k) for 6‑month pilots. Budget breakdown included.
